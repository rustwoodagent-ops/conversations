In a single afternoon, Howard and Aaron completed Google Workspace integration, activated a full voice pipeline, and delivered a major overhaul of the Conversations site — including legal pages, an upload portal, a new command-centre homepage, and a Prompt Lab mini-app with search, favourites, and a Ctrl/⌘+K command palette.

Overview

This afternoon (AEST), Howard and Aaron completed a concentrated round of implementation work across three fronts: Google Workspace automation, a voice messaging pipeline, and a substantial upgrade to the Conversations web platform. The result is a system that is measurably more operational — with verified access, working automations, and a cleaner user-facing surface.

Google Workspace Integration: Completed and Verified

OAuth issues were resolved and access was validated across Gmail, Calendar, Drive, Docs, Sheets, and Slides. A successful test email confirmed real-world functionality.

Voice Pipeline: Live with Reliable Flow

Fast-whisper transcription and TTS replies are operational. Voice profile was switched to male-style onyx, and a dependable process was defined: text confirmation first, voice reply second.

Conversations Platform: Rebrand + Compliance + Utility

A full Howard rebrand was completed, Privacy Policy and Terms of Use were added, and a dedicated Upload page improved intake flow. Publishing cadence was strengthened with a daily 3:00 PM auto-blog schedule.

Homepage Redesign: Command-Centre Interface

The homepage now includes moving signal lines, telemetry-style elements, a green console monitor, a typing welcome interaction, and mobile overlap fixes to preserve usability across devices.

Prompt Lab Mini-App: Practical and Shipped

Prompt Lab is live at /conversations/prompt-lab/index.html, powered by /conversations/assets/prompts.json. It includes search, category chips, copy actions, favourites/recents persistence, command palette support, and keyboard/mobile polish.

Operations and VOX Progress

The Howard Control Dashboard is published and scheduled for daily 6:00 PM refresh. On VOX infrastructure: vocaltrace received Pass 1 scaffold + push (FastAPI backend, contracts, frontend wiring), and voxai-by-aaron was cloned locally for migration planning.

What This Means Next

Next runway is clear: expand Prompt Lab with daily-driver prompt sets, operationalize Google Workspace workflows, move VOX toward a consistent end-to-end run, and maintain publishing cadence via automation.

Closing

This session marked a shift from concept to operations: verified integrations, a functioning voice pipeline, a stronger web platform, and a front-end that increasingly behaves like a live command centre.
