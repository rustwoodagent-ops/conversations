About Aaron Ellis

I'm a vocal coach, producer, and systems architect. My work explores the intersection of human creativity, musical craftsmanship, and thoughtful technology.

The Rustwood Practice

Rustwood is my creative studio and coaching practice. It's built on a simple philosophy: help people unlock their voice through clarity, structure, and human connection.

Whether I'm producing a custom song, coaching someone through a vocal challenge, or designing systems for creative work, I bring the same discipline: clear outcomes, evidence-based method, and respect for the person I'm working with.

What I Do

Vocal Coaching

One-on-one sessions designed to develop your technique, expand your range, and unlock confidence in your voice.

Custom Song Production

I create original songs tailored to your voice and story. Fast turnaround, unlimited revisions, collaborative process.

Creative Direction

Strategic guidance on arrangement, production, songwriting, and finding your artistic voice.

Systems Design

Behind the scenes, I build systems for creative work—measurement frameworks, workflow automation, and human-centered AI integration.

Philosophy

Human-first. Technology and systems should amplify human capability, not replace it. Coaching is about the person first.

Clear outcomes. Vague goals lead to vague results. Every engagement starts with explicit definition of what success looks like.

Verifiable results. I don't claim value I can't measure. Whether it's vocal range expansion, song quality, or system efficiency, outcomes are tracked and visible.

Structural thinking. Most creative challenges aren't solved by effort alone—they're solved by better structure. I help identify and build the right frameworks.

Background

I've spent the last several years working at the intersection of AI orchestration, business automation, and vocal pedagogy. My background spans music production, prompt engineering, and building systems that let humans and AI collaborate effectively.

On the technical side, I've architected multi-agent AI systems, designed operational dashboards for complex workflows, and built telemetry frameworks that turn execution into measurable data.

On the creative side, I'm obsessed with vocal technique, songwriting craft, and the psychology of creative confidence.
