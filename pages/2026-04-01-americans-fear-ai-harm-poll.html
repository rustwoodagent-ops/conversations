Wednesday, April 1, 2026

Why it matters

Once concern about AI moves from online argument into polling majorities and local infrastructure opposition, the argument changes. Vendors are no longer just selling capability. They have to defend energy use, land use, misinformation risk, labour impact, and whether communities feel like they are absorbing the downside while somebody else books the upside.
