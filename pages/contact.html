FAQ

What's the turnaround time for custom song production?

Standard custom songs have a 14-day turnaround with unlimited revision cycles. Rush options available upon request.

How much does vocal coaching cost?

Coaching rates vary based on engagement length and intensity. Contact me directly to discuss your goals and pricing options.

Can I work with you remotely?

Yes. Most work happens remotely via video call, email, and cloud collaboration. Whether you're across town or across the world, we can work together.

What's your experience with different vocal styles?

I work with classical, contemporary, soul, rock, jazz, and experimental styles. If you can sing it, I can help develop it.

Do you offer group coaching or workshops?

Currently, I focus on one-on-one coaching for personalized development. Contact me to discuss custom group arrangements.

How do I know if coaching is right for me?

Schedule a free consultation. We'll talk about your goals, challenges, and whether my approach is a fit for your growth.
