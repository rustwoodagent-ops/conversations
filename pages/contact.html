FAQ

What should I email about?

Email is the right path for availability questions, collaboration ideas, Operator OS interest, Howard workflow questions, or help finding the right Rustwood page or resource.

Is public checkout live for everything on Rustwood?

No. Public offers are limited to what can be presented truthfully right now. Where checkout is not verified, the site uses preview, early-access, or direct-contact paths instead.

How do I join Rustwood Operator OS early access?

Use the dedicated early-access page. It prepares a direct email draft so interest is only recorded when you actually send it.

Can I ask about a custom AI workflow or system build?

Yes. Send a concise email with your current setup, the bottleneck, and the outcome you want. That makes it much easier to assess fit and next steps.

Where can I browse current public material first?

Start with the Archive for published material and the Offers page for current public resources, previews, and early-access routes.

What if I am not sure which page or offer is relevant?

Email directly and describe what you are trying to do. It is better to route you manually than send you into a misleading or half-ready public flow.
