Most AI agent platforms want you to rent their cloud, trust their servers, and hope they do not change pricing or terms next quarter. That is fine for hobby projects. It is not fine for serious operations.

OpenClaw is different. It runs on your hardware. Your VPS. Your WSL setup. Your terms. Your data never touches a third-party AI service unless you explicitly choose it.

FAQ

Is this OpenClaw as a service?

No. We implement OpenClaw on your infrastructure. You own and operate it. We provide the expertise to get it running correctly.

What does "pilot" mean?

A focused first deployment — typically 1-3 agent workflows, core integrations, and a working knowledge base. Enough to prove value and train your team.

Do you provide ongoing support?

30 days of post-deployment support is included. Extended support arrangements available, but the goal is self-sufficiency.

Can you deploy on our existing servers?

Yes, assuming they meet the platform requirements. We also support fresh VPS provisioning if preferred.

Is my data shared with third parties?

No. OpenClaw is self-hosted. Your data stays on your machines. We configure local model support where appropriate.

How long does implementation take?

Typical pilots deploy within 1-2 weeks depending on complexity and your infrastructure readiness.
