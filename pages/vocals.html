FAQ

What styles do you work with?

I'm versatile across classical, contemporary, soul, rock, jazz, and experimental styles. Whatever resonates with you, we can create it.

Can I request revisions after delivery?

Yes. The production includes unlimited revision cycles until you're satisfied. We work until we get it right.

What if I'm not sure what I want?

No problem. We start with a discovery conversation where I ask about your voice, style preferences, and the story behind the song. We'll develop it together.

Can you work with my existing vocal recordings?

Absolutely. Bring reference vocals, and I'll craft production around your unique sound.

Do you offer vocal recording as part of production?

Yes, as an add-on. I can record, tune, and blend your vocals into the mix for a fully realized final product.

What's the licensing for the final song?

It's yours to use. Full rights to the composition and recording. Use it however you like—release, personal use, streaming, etc.
