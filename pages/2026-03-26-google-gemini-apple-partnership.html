Google Gemini Hits 750 Million Users as Apple Partnership Deepens

26 March 2026 · Howard News Hub

🎧 Listen to This Story

Three-quarters of a billion people now use Google Gemini. Let that number sink in. That's more than double the population of the United States. And it's not just a chatbot anymore—Google is transforming Gemini into what they call an "ambient intelligence layer" that anticipates needs and integrates seamlessly across the entire digital environment.

The Apple Connection

Here's the plot twist that would have seemed impossible a year ago: Apple now has full access to Google's Gemini models to customize them for Siri and on-device AI. This isn't just an API partnership. Apple can create smaller, efficient models with Gemini-like performance specifically optimized for iPhones, iPads, and Macs.

The implications are enormous. Siri has been the punchline of AI assistant jokes for years. With Gemini under the hood, that changes. Apple gets world-class language models without building them from scratch. Google gets their technology embedded in over a billion devices. Users get an actually competent Siri. Everyone wins—except maybe OpenAI, who just lost their spot as Apple's rumored partner.

The March Feature Drop

While the Apple news grabbed headlines, Google shipped a flurry of other updates in March 2026:

Gemini-embedding-2-preview is now multimodal, handling text, images, video, audio, and PDFs in a single model. That's a big deal for anyone building search or retrieval systems.

Lyria 3 music generation models can now produce 48kHz stereo audio from text and image inputs. Musicians, start your (potentially concerned) engines.

Workspace Studio lets users build multi-step AI workflows without code. Think Zapier, but actually intelligent.

Google TV integration brings richer visual assistance, deep dives into topics, and sports briefs to your living room.

Task automations on Pixel and Samsung phones can now order Uber or DoorDash through voice commands. Small thing, but it hints at where this is going.

The Strategy

Google isn't trying to win with a single killer feature. They're playing the integration game. Gemini is everywhere: Search, Docs, Gmail, Photos, Maps, TV, your phone. The model improvements matter, but the distribution matters more. When your AI is already where users are, you don't need to convince them to adopt it.

The "ambient intelligence" framing is telling. Google wants Gemini to be the invisible layer that makes everything else work better—not a destination you visit, but the air you breathe digitally.

What This Means for the AI Wars

OpenAI has the best models. Anthropic has the strongest safety brand. But Google has distribution that neither can touch. With 750 million users and growing, Gemini's reach is becoming a competitive moat all its own.

The Apple partnership accelerates this. If Gemini-powered Siri actually works well, that's hundreds of millions more users exposed to Google's AI daily. The network effects compound. The data flywheel spins faster.

For competitors, the window for disruption is closing. For users, the future looks increasingly like one where Google AI is simply... everywhere.
