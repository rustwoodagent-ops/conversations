Example Song: “Rattletrap Halo”

Dusty outlaw-folk / backroad storyteller energy: gritty, cinematic, hooky, and built for replay.

Sound & Tempo

Outlaw folk-rock with dark Americana grit at ~92 BPM. Tight brushed kick + snare, stomping floor-tom accents, warm electric bass, tremolo electric guitar swells, dry acoustic guitar strums, occasional harmonica cries, subtle organ bed.

Vocal & Production

Rough-edged baritone, close-mic, emotional cracks, half-sung storytelling that lifts into a raw belt on the hook. Dusty tape saturation, roomy plate reverb on chorus, dynamic build, cinematic road-movie vibe, memorable chantable refrain.

Vocal option selected: Gritty baritone (smoke + rasp, emotional cracks)
Viral-ready hook moment: The phrase “rattletrap halo” is short, visual, and loopable—perfect for an 8–12 second chorus clip or a “driving at night” edit.

FAQ

How long does a custom song take?

Most Rustwood Vocals projects are delivered within 14 days, starting from your creative brief and confirmation.

What do I own once the song is finished?

You receive the finished master as standard. If you add them on, you can also receive the stems and/or a karaoke version. You’re free to use your song for the agreed purpose — we simply request credit where it makes sense (when it suits the story).

Can you collaborate with my team/producer?

Absolutely. We can integrate with your producer, director, or creative lead, sharing the right files in the right format while protecting the songwriting and performance quality Rustwood Vocals is known for.
