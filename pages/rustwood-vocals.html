A Boutique Approach to Your Music.

Welcome to Rustwood Vocals. Founded and operated by Aaron, we are a boutique, professional custom song production service dedicated to the art of songwriting.

As a passionate music creator, Aaron's mission is simple: to help singers and writers transform their rough, written lyrics into fully realised, beautifully produced songs.

Whether you have a poem hidden in a notebook or a melody stuck in your head, we provide a warm, collaborative, and highly professional environment to bring your vision into the real world.

The Creative Process

A clear, collaborative journey from written word to final track.

01

Submit Your Lyrics

It all starts with your words. Send over your rough lyrics, poems, or ideas. They don't have to be perfect—that's what we're here for.

02

Refinement & Structure

We refine your lyrics into a proper song structure (verses, chorus, bridge). Minor wording adjustments may be made to ensure perfect flow and singability.

03

Define the Vibe

You tell us exactly what you're looking for by providing:

  • Mood and emotional feel
  • Genre (dance, rap, ballad, rock, etc.)
  • Vocal style (soft, gritty, sweet, powerful)
  • Your vocal range (if you plan to sing it)
04

Custom Previews

Aaron hits the studio. We will create two distinct custom preview versions (partial tracks) and send them directly to your email for review.

05

Select Your Version

Listen to the partial previews and choose the direction that best captures the heart and soul of your original lyrics.

06

Final Delivery

Upon purchase, the final, fully-produced, high-quality track is completed, mixed, and delivered securely to you.

Commercial Rights

100% Yours to Keep.

We believe your art belongs to you. Once the final track is delivered, you own it completely. You are granted full commercial rights, meaning you may distribute, perform, stream, or use your new song however you wish, forever.

Optional Upgrade

Take the Stage.

Planning to perform your new song live? We offer an exclusive add-on service to remove the lead studio vocals and create a pristine, professional karaoke backing track. Perfect for live performances, open mics, or local karaoke venues.
