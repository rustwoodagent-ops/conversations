A premium songwriting partner, built around your voice.

Rustwood Vocals is a custom production service founded and operated by Aaron. The mission is simple: transform rough lyrics, notebook poems, and melody ideas into emotionally strong, professionally produced songs.

You bring the story. We shape the structure, define the sonic direction, and deliver a final track you can confidently own, share, and perform.

The Creative Process

A clear, staged flow from raw lyric concept to finished record.

STEP 01

Submit Your Lyrics

Share your words, notes, poem, or draft. Perfection is not required.

STEP 02

Refinement & Structure

Lyrics are refined into a high-flow structure: verse, chorus, bridge, and momentum.

STEP 03

Define the Vibe

  • Mood and emotional tone
  • Genre direction
  • Vocal delivery style
  • Range considerations (if you’ll sing it)
STEP 04

Custom Previews

Two distinct partial versions are produced and sent for review.

STEP 05

Select Direction

Choose the version that best captures your song’s emotional center.

STEP 06

Final Delivery

Final production, polish, and secure high-quality delivery after purchase.
