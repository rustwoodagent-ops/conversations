## A Korean Startup Claims It Can Beat Nvidia by 5x

Korean startup Hyper Accel announced the Bertha 500 AI chip for LLM inference, claiming up to 5x efficiency over Nvidia GPUs and 20x throughput per dollar. Built on Samsung 4nm process, co-developed with Naver Cloud, uses LPDDR memory and dataflow-optimized design. Samples released, mass production targeted for early 2027.

**Key Facts:**
- Announced March 18, 2026
- Targets data center inference market
- Bring-up phase: September 2026
- Mass production: Early 2027
- Joins Rebellions and FuriosaAI in challenging Nvidia
