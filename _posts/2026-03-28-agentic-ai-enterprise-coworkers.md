# Agentic AI: 40% of Enterprise Apps Will Have Digital Coworkers by Year End

Agentic AI has arrived, and it's coming for your workplace. By year end, 40% of enterprise applications will feature digital coworkers—autonomous AI agents that don't just assist but actively execute tasks, make decisions, and collaborate alongside human teams.

## The Players

Microsoft Copilot Cowork, Nvidia's Nemotron 3, and a swarm of startups are betting the future of work is human-AI collaboration.

## Not Chatbots

These aren't chatbots. They're digital colleagues with:
- Persistent memory
- Contextual awareness
- Ability to act independently
- Integration with enterprise systems
- Decision-making capabilities

## Welcome to the Era of Digital Coworkers

The age of passive AI is over. Welcome to the era of digital coworkers—AI agents that will fundamentally reshape how enterprises operate.

Organizations need to prepare for a workforce where humans and AI agents collaborate as peers, not as master and tool.
