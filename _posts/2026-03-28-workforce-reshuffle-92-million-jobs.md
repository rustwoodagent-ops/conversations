# The Great Workforce Reshuffle: 92 Million Jobs at Risk by 2030

92 million jobs. That's how many positions the World Economic Forum predicts will be displaced by AI by 2030.

## But Here's the Twist

It's not all doom and gloom. The same report forecasts AI will create 170 million new roles, netting 78 million additional jobs globally.

## The Catch

The jobs being created look nothing like the ones being lost.

Routine cognitive work is evaporating while AI-augmented creative, strategic, and emotionally intelligent roles are exploding.

![Human-AI collaboration in modern workplace](/conversations/assets/images/2026-03-28-workforce-reshuffle-92-million-jobs-inline.png)

## The Winners

The winners won't be those who compete with AI, but those who learn to dance with it.

Key skills for the future:
- Creative problem-solving
- Emotional intelligence
- Strategic thinking
- AI collaboration and prompt engineering
- Adaptability and continuous learning

The great reshuffle is coming. Will you be ready?
