OpenAI released GPT-5.4 mini and nano, but these aren't just incremental updates. They signal the shift from monolithic AI to orchestrated AI teams. GPT-5.4 mini is over twice as fast as its predecessor while delivering near-full model performance on benchmarks. The nano variant is API-only, designed for high-speed, low-cost tasks like classification and summarization. OpenAI is positioning these as team members, not replacements. Larger models handle complex planning, mini models execute specific subtasks, and nano handles high-volume operations. This is how human teams work, and now it's how AI systems will work too. The strategy is clear: own every point on the quality, speed, and cost curve.
