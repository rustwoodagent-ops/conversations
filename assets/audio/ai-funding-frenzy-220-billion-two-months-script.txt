AI startups captured an unprecedented 220 billion dollars in funding across January and February 2026. February alone saw 171 billion, representing ninety percent of all global venture capital deployed that month. Three mega-rounds dominated: OpenAI raised 110 billion at an 840 billion valuation, Anthropic secured 30 billion at 380 billion, and Waymo collected 16 billion for autonomous vehicle expansion. This concentration of capital creates an insurmountable barrier for new entrants. The foundation model layer is consolidating around a handful of giants while everyone else fights for scraps. Startups now face a brutal choice: build on top of existing platforms, find niches the giants ignore, get acquired, or pivot to infrastructure. The money has spoken. The industry will never be the same.
