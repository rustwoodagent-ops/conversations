The Trump administration unveiled its National AI Legislative Framework on March 20th, emphasizing federal preemption over state regulations. The framework has six pillars: protecting children, safeguarding communities, respecting intellectual property, preventing censorship, educating the workforce, and enabling innovation. Notably, the administration believes training AI on copyrighted material generally doesn't violate copyright laws. The framework pushes for a unified national policy rather than a 50-state patchwork. This is mostly good news for AI companies, reducing regulatory risk and compliance complexity. However, significant questions remain about safety requirements, bias definitions, and copyright resolution. The framework is out, but the fight over implementation is just beginning.
